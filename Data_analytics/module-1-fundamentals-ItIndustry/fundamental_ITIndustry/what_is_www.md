# What is the WWW ?

1. The World Wide Web (WWW) is a system of interconnected documents and other resources, linked by hyperlinks and URLs. It is a subset of the Internet and represents the most commonly used application of the Internet. The WWW was invented by Tim Berners-Lee in 1989 while he was working at CERN, the European Organization for Nuclear Research. The goal was to create a decentralized system for sharing information across the globe.

2. The WWW operates on a client-server model, where web browsers (clients) request information from web servers, which then respond with the requested content. This content is typically written in HTML (Hypertext Markup Language) and can include text, images, videos, and other multimedia elements.

3. The WWW has revolutionized how people communicate, access information, and conduct business. It has become an integral part of modern life, with billions of users around the world relying on it for various purposes such as education, entertainment, commerce, and social interaction.
